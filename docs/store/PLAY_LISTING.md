## Full description (max 4000)

```
Eigen is a calm home screen for Android. Your apps live in drawers, your messages sit on the left, your widgets on the right, and an assistant runs on the phone. No ads, no analytics, no account, and nothing you type leaves the phone.

POWER WITHOUT CLUTTER
• Gestures: double-tap to lock, swipe for notifications or quick settings, two-finger swipes, Home again
• Search finds apps, shortcuts, contacts, Android settings, sums and unit conversions
• Icon shapes, themed icons, and any icon from any pack for a single app
• Private apps behind your fingerprint or PIN (Plus)
• Share your whole setup as one small file
• Widget stacks that turn to what's playing or your next meeting

HOME, YOUR WAY
• A big clock, then your drawers by name. Tap one to open it into a grid of cards
• Swipe up for every app, A to Z, with a letter scroller
• One search capsule for every app, work profile included
• Hold an app for shortcuts, its dashboard, the dock, favourites, renaming, hiding or uninstalling
• A dock for up to four apps

WIDGETS PAGE
Swipe left for Now playing, Next alarm, Agenda, Day, Battery and any widget from your apps. They stack from the bottom, within reach of your thumb.

CONTEXTS
The widgets page and the drawer Home opens on can follow what you are doing: days, a time window, headphones, charging, Do Not Disturb or media playing. With Plus: battery level, Wi-Fi, a meeting in your calendar or your car's Bluetooth, and a context can change your colour or run a Tasker task. Tasker and MacroDroid can hold contexts and hear when they change. Contexts are only checked while Home is on screen.

CONVERSATIONS AND THE ASSISTANT
Swipe right for Conversations, one timeline of the messaging apps you choose, with inline replies. It needs notification access and reads only what those notifications already show, on your phone. The assistant uses Gemini Nano through Android AICore where your phone has it, and quick on-device answers everywhere else. It sets timers and alarms, plays music, starts directions, dials people and catches you up on your messages, all on the phone.

YOUR LOOK
Five colours, six clock styles, your own photo or Eigen's textures, Frost or Liquid glass, icon packs, and text that stays readable over any wallpaper.

EIGEN PLUS
Eigen is a complete launcher for free. Eigen Plus is a one-time purchase, no subscription, that adds unlimited contexts with smarter triggers, automation, private apps, more clocks and textures (including ones that follow the day), extra card styles, more widgets and Conversations. Try Plus clocks and card styles before you buy. You can also leave a tip in Settings; tips are separate and unlock nothing.

PRIVATE
Everything stays on your phone. No ads, no analytics. Eigen goes online only to download an assistant model you pick, when the phone has no Gemini Nano and no other 72F app already has one. Purchases and tips go through the Play Store app.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Eigen's optional gesture service uses Android's Accessibility API only to lock the screen, open notifications or quick settings, and show recent apps when you make a gesture you set. It doesn't read your screen, observe other apps or collect any data. It's off until you turn it on.
```
