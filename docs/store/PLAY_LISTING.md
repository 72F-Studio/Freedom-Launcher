## Full description (max 4000)

```
Eigen is a calm home screen for Android. Your apps live in drawers, your messages sit on the left, your widgets on the right, and an assistant runs on the phone. No ads, no analytics, no account, and no internet permission.

HOME, YOUR WAY
• A big clock, then your drawers by name. Tap one to open it into a grid of cards
• Swipe up for every app, A to Z, with a letter scroller
• One search capsule for every app, work profile included
• Hold an app for shortcuts, its dashboard, the dock, favourites, renaming, hiding or uninstalling
• A dock for up to four apps

WIDGETS PAGE
Swipe left for Now playing, Next alarm, Agenda, Day, Battery and any widget from your apps. They stack from the bottom, within reach of your thumb.

CONTEXTS
The widgets page and the drawer Home opens on can follow what you are doing: days, a time window, headphones, charging, Do Not Disturb or media playing. Contexts are only checked while Home is on screen.

CONVERSATIONS AND THE ASSISTANT
Swipe right for Conversations, one timeline of the messaging apps you choose, with inline replies. It needs notification access and reads only what those notifications already show, on your phone. The assistant uses Gemini Nano through Android AICore where your phone has it, and quick on-device answers everywhere else.

YOUR LOOK
Five colours, six clock styles, your own photo or Eigen's textures, Frost or Liquid glass, icon packs, and text that stays readable over any wallpaper.

EIGEN PLUS
Eigen is a complete launcher for free. Eigen Plus is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited contexts, more clocks and textures, extra card styles, more widgets and Conversations. Try Plus clocks and card styles before you buy. You can also leave a tip in Settings; tips are separate and unlock nothing.

PRIVATE
Everything stays on your phone. Eigen has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics. Purchases and tips go through the Play Store app.
```
