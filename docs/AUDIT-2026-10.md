# Eigen Launcher audit, October 2026

Reference: a 5-minute screen recording of Ratio 7.2.0 (onboarding, home, drawers, search, Conversations, Root, settings). Key frames are in `docs/ratio-reference/`.

## What Ratio gets right

| Area | What Ratio does | Why it feels expensive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Nothing but four centred drawer names in heavy 30sp type over a blurred, greyed wallpaper. Tap one and it opens in place into a 5-column grid of vertical cards. | One idea per screen. No chrome, no pills, no counters. |
| Tiles | Vertical cards: icon on top, label under it, ~6% white fill, ~12dp corners, no outline, 4dp gaps. | The fill is barely there, so the wallpaper carries the colour. |
| Search | A single frosted pill pinned to the bottom ("Tap to search" + magnifier). Results stack upward above it, nearest the thumb. | The thumb never travels. |
| Pages | Left: Conversations. Centre: home. Right: Root (widgets). | Each page has one job. |
| Sheets | Everything rises from the bottom with a grab handle, a centred bold title and one white pill CTA. | Consistent and calm. |
| Settings | Huge bold section names that expand in place into small rows. | Reads like a table of contents. |
| Colour | Strictly greyscale; colour only from wallpaper and (optionally) icons. | Restraint. |
| Motion | Drawers grow open, items fade/slide in, sheets spring. | Constant soft motion makes it feel alive. |

## Direction

- **Ratio's elegance, Kelvin's material.** Ratio's layout and type hierarchy; Kelvin's frosted glass, rim-lit pebbles, springs and Onest-style weight.
- **Colour is opt-in to remove.** Default is *Kesar*: a saffron/motichoor-ladoo orange (`#FF8A1F`) accent with original icon colours. *Greyscale* mode turns icons and accent monochrome and desaturates the in-launcher wallpaper, the way Ratio does.
- **HyperOS-strength blur** on the in-launcher wallpaper (blur 32 by default), with the dim set low so colour survives. System wallpaper can't be blurred by us; that is said plainly in the wallpaper menu.
- **Clock styles:** Ratio (heavy), Thin, Stacked, Outline, Kesar split (minutes in accent), Binary.

## Done in this pass

- **Kotlin + Compose + Kelvin.** Kelvin is vendored in `design/kelvin` (commit in `SYNCED_FROM`) and built by the `:kelvin` module, as in 72F-Gallery and AppGone. `MainActivity` (Java) is now a `ComponentActivity` that hosts one `ComposeView`; it still owns app discovery, widget hosting, settings and dialogs, and feeds `EigenState`.
- **Home** (`ui/Home.kt`): centred clock, Ratio-style centred drawer names that open in place into 5-column vertical cards rising in one after another, the opened drawer scrolls into view, one Kelvin glass search capsule at the bottom.
- **Clock styles** (`ui/Clock.kt`): Ratio, Thin, Stacked, Outline, Kesar split, Binary.
- **Search**: the capsule becomes the field in place; results stack upward by the thumb; natural-language queries get an on-device answer card that streams in and hands off to the Assistant.
- **Left page** (`ui/LeftPage.kt`): Conversations (inline reply, open, dismiss) and Assistant on a Kelvin segmented control.
- **Right page** (`ui/WidgetsPage.kt`): now playing, next alarm, agenda, day progress, notes, hosted app widgets. The fake weather card is gone.
- **On-device AI** (`ai/MlKitEngine.kt`, `ai/EigenAssistant.kt`): Tofu's ML Kit GenAI Prompt engine (Gemini Nano through AICore), streamed, plus quick answers that need no model. Cloud Gemini code, LiteRT and the `INTERNET` permission are gone.
- **Colour**: Kesar (`#FF8A1F`) is the default accent, with Fire, Ladoo, Haldi and Greyscale in Colour settings. Greyscale uses Kelvin's Graphite, monochrome icons and a desaturated wallpaper.
- **Wallpaper**: blur 32 with a little extra saturation, a lighter default dim (35%), and "Set as phone wallpaper" (home, lock or both).
